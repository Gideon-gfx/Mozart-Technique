How Access Codes Work

Individual Sponsor: Once approved, you can generate multiple access codes for individual students. Each code can be used by one student to join the platform.

NGO/Institution: Once approved and after subscription payment, you'll generate a single organizational code. All teachers and students at your organization share this one code to join under your sponsorship.

💡 This single code shows all users that they're affiliated with your organization.
