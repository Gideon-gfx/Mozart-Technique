Account & Data

Delete Your Account

Mozart Techniques is an online platform connecting music students with tutors, performers and sponsoring organizations, available on the web at mozarttechniques.com and as an Android/iOS app. This page explains how to request deletion of your Mozart Techniques account, and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

Last updated: September 2026

1. How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your account in either of these ways:

  • In the app or on the website: Sign in, open your Profile menu, and choose Delete Account. This sends the same request described below.
  • From this page: If you can't sign in, or no longer have the app installed, submit the form below with the name and email on your account. You don't need to log in or install anything to use it.

Every request is reviewed and completed by our team - it isn't fully automated - so we can verify it's really you and safely unwind anything tied to active lessons, bookings or sponsorships first.

2. Request Deletion

We'll email you at the address above to confirm your request has been received, and again once it's completed.

3. What Gets Deleted

Once your identity is verified, we permanently delete:

Deleted

  • Your profile: name, photo, bio, phone number and email
  • Messages and chat history, including voice notes and shared files
  • Uploaded videos, recordings and documents (teaching library, submissions, portfolios)
  • Saved addresses, location data and device push tokens
  • Tutor, student, performer or sponsor profile details and preferences
  • Your account credentials and login access

Retained

  • Payment and transaction records, as required for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations
  • Records needed to resolve an open dispute, refund, chargeback or investigation you're already part of
  • Information we're legally required to keep under applicable law
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that no longer identifies you

Reviews and ratings you've left on other users' profiles are de-identified (shown as "Former user") rather than deleted outright, since removing them would also erase the other person's rating history.

4. How Long It Takes

We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once your data has been removed. If your account has an active subscription, sponsored student, or open booking, we'll reach out first to help you wrap that up before deleting anything tied to it.

5. Questions

For anything not covered here, see our full Privacy Policy or reach us through the Contact page at mozarttechniques@gmail.com.
